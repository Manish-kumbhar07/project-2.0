System Architecture & Methodology

CentralSaathi is engineered as a deterministic transit intelligence analyzer for Mumbai's Central Line network. Unlike generic routing services that interpolate approximate road geometries, CentralSaathi operates directly on real railway topology:

  • Relational SQLite Topology: Stores 49 physical stations, quadruple-track trackage order, and timetable services in SQLite.
  • Sequence-Validated Routing: Computes verified travel sequences, avoiding synthetic paths and accurately reflecting Fast vs Slow stop patterns.
  • Real Service Density: Directly aggregates departures and arrivals across hourly intervals from timetable tables without artificial random distributions.
  • Corridor Disruption Matching: Identifies active maintenance blocks and track advisories affecting specific stations along the commuter path.
